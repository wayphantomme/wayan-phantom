# Industry Domains

How technical patterns from fullstack, Web3, and AI apply to specific industries. Each section focuses on *how to build it* — data models, system design, and implementation patterns — not just what the domain is.

---

## Why Domain Knowledge Matters

A senior developer who understands the business context builds better systems. Knowing that a healthcare app needs an immutable audit trail, or that a finance ledger must be double-entry, changes the architecture from the start — not after the first audit.
