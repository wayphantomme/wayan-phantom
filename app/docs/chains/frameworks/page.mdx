# Chain Frameworks

Three production-grade frameworks for deploying your own chain. Pick based on which ecosystem you want to settle on.

---

## Overview

| Framework | Settle On | Type | Best For |
|-----------|-----------|------|----------|
| Arbitrum Orbit | Arbitrum One or Nova | Optimistic rollup L3 | App-specific chains on Arbitrum |
| OP Stack | Ethereum L1 | Optimistic rollup L2 | Joining the Superchain |
| Substrate | Polkadot (or standalone) | Custom chain | Full control, Polkadot ecosystem |

---

## Which to Choose

**Use Arbitrum Orbit** if: you want an L3, your users are already on Arbitrum, you want Arbitrum's liquidity and tooling.

**Use OP Stack** if: you want an L2, you want to be part of the Superchain, Coinbase ecosystem matters to you.

**Use Substrate** if: you want maximum customization, you want to become a Polkadot parachain, or you're building something that doesn't fit the EVM model.
